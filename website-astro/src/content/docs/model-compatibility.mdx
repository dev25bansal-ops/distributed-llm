## Supported families

Any Hugging Face causal-LM with a standard `AutoModelForCausalLM` head works:
Llama 1/2/3, Mistral, Qwen 1.5/2/2.5, Phi-2/3, Gemma, TinyStories, GPT-2, and
most fine-tunes of these.

## VRAM guidance (float16 weights)

| Model | Weights | KV cache (4k ctx) | Recommended |
|-------|---------|-------------------|-------------|
| ≤1B | ~2 GB | ~0.5 GB | Any GPU or CPU |
| 3B | ~6 GB | ~1 GB | 8 GB GPU |
| 7–8B | ~14 GB | ~2 GB | 16 GB GPU or 2-node split |
| 13B | ~26 GB | ~3 GB | 2× 16 GB pooled |
| 70B | ~140 GB | ~12 GB | 5–6× 24 GB pooled |

INT8 halves weight memory; INT4 quarters it. Quantized KV caches reduce
context memory further — the bulk-dequantization path keeps long-context
generation fast.
