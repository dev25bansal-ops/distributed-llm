LlamaIndex

by LlamaIndex
Official

RAG pipelines with DistLLM backend. Supports streaming and embeddings.

⭐ 1.8k 📦 32k/mo

CrewAI

Multi-agent orchestration with DistLLM as the underlying LLM provider.
